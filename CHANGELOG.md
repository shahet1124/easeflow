## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-27

First public release.

### Added

- **Workflow** (`SKILL.md`), in phases 0–8: set up, inventory everything, choose personas and tasks, blind walkthrough, verify and score, redesign, prototype and blind re-test, deliver, and implement on approval.
- **12 personas**: Harold (the gold standard), the Literal Follower, Alex (power-user guardrail), Priya, Ramesh, Frank, Linda, Grace, Ana, Jay, Margaret and Sam, plus a template for new ones.
- **Feature coverage**: complete feature inventory, completeness check, jobs tiered Core/Regular/Advanced, and a feature ledger with a 100% coverage gate.
- **Complexity handling**: the disclosure ladder and evidence tests for when a global Advanced view is justified.
- **Scoring**: per-task and per-persona scores (0–100), pass/fail, and the product verdict.
- **28 simplification rules** (R1–R28).
- **Safeguards against over-optimistic AI walkthroughs**: blind walkers, the four cognitive-walkthrough questions, default to fail, an evidence check on findings, two Harold runs, measured accessibility checks, and a blind re-test.
- **Templates**: a product-themed prototype skeleton (browser, phone and window frames; More options; Advanced view) and a before/after showcase page.
- **Real-user test kit** for checking results with five real people.
- Plugin manifest and marketplace file for installing from GitHub.
