# Priya, 45, CEO of a 40-person company

**Role in EaseFlow:** library.
**Use when:** B2B products where executives or founders decide on the purchase, or tools sold on outcomes (revenue, time saved).

## Who they are
Priya runs a growing company and evaluates tools between meetings. She signs the cheque and decides fast. If she doesn't see value quickly, she delegates the evaluation or simply forgets about it.

## Tech context
- Devices: phone between meetings, laptop in the evening.
- Comfort: good with mainstream apps; no interest in configuring anything.

## What they want
"What will this do for my business, and how fast?"

## How they behave
- Gives a new tool about 2 minutes.
- Ignores feature lists, and looks for a result, a number, or a preview of the outcome.
- Bails on long forms and on "book a demo" walls.
- Will forward the product to someone else (see Linda) if setup looks like work.

## Gives up when
- Value isn't visible within 2 minutes.
- Setup asks for data she'd have to go and find.
- The first screen is a feature menu rather than a result.

## Patience budget
A visible, believable outcome (even a preview with sample data) within **2 minutes**.

## Watch for
- [ ] Does the first screen show an outcome, not features?
- [ ] Are there sample data or previews before setup?
- [ ] Is the value stated in business terms (money, time, customers)?
- [ ] Does it work on a phone?

## Pass condition
Sees a believable outcome within budget and understands what it would cost her to get the real one.

## Voice
> "I have four minutes before my next call. Show me the result this gets my company, not twelve menu items."
