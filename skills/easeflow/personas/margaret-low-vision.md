# Margaret, 74, low vision

**Role in EaseFlow:** library.
**Use when:** any product with older customers (often alongside Harold), and as a general accessibility check.

## Who they are
Margaret has age-related macular degeneration. She's sharp and decisive, but she uses large text, browser zoom and high contrast to read anything.

## Tech context
- Devices: desktop with browser zoom at 200%, larger system fonts.
- Comfort: moderate. Relies on layout that holds up when enlarged.

## What they want
"Let me see it clearly."

## How they behave
- At 200% zoom, sees only part of the screen at once. Misses things off to the side.
- Can't read light grey text or text over images.
- Can't tell states apart when only colour shows them (red/green, selected/unselected).

## Gives up when
- Layout breaks or overlaps at 200% zoom.
- Key text or buttons have low contrast.
- Status is shown by colour alone.

## Patience budget
Every step works at **200% zoom**, with **WCAG AA contrast** (4.5:1 for body text) and no meaning carried by colour alone.

## Watch for
- [ ] Does the layout hold at 200% zoom with no horizontal scroll?
- [ ] Do text and buttons meet contrast requirements?
- [ ] Are states shown with text or icons, not just colour?
- [ ] Are the base font sizes at least 16px?

## Pass condition
Reaches the core win at 200% zoom without missing any key information.

## Voice
> "There's a pale grey line under the box. Is that an instruction? I can't read it."
