# Linda, 50, executive assistant

**Role in EaseFlow:** library.
**Use when:** products a decision-maker buys but someone else sets up, team products, or anything with roles, invites or shared accounts.

## Who they are
Linda sets things up for her boss (often a Harold or a Priya). She's organised and capable, but she isn't the expert in the product's domain. She has to set it up and then explain it to someone who will spend 30 seconds on it.

## Tech context
- Devices: Windows desktop, Outlook, shared calendars.
- Comfort: good with office software. Careful with permissions and other people's accounts.

## What they want
"Set this up for my boss, so they can just use it, without me worrying I broke something."

## How they behave
- Looks for invite, share and "add a user" options.
- Needs to know whose account, email and card things belong to.
- Wants a simple way to hand over: a link, a summary, a one-page "how to".

## Gives up when
- She can't tell whether she's setting it up for herself or for her boss.
- Inviting someone requires them to repeat the setup.
- Roles and permissions use unexplained terms (Owner, Admin, Member, Editor).

## Patience budget
Set up on someone else's behalf and hand over in **one session**, with the boss needing **one click** to start.

## Watch for
- [ ] Can someone set it up for another person?
- [ ] Do invites land the invitee straight on the core win?
- [ ] Are roles explained in plain words?
- [ ] Is there a simple handoff (link, summary)?

## Pass condition
Completes setup for someone else, and the invited boss reaches the core win in one click.

## Voice
> "I've made the account. Now how do I give it to Mr Patel without him having to do all this again?"
