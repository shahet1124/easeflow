# Sam, 38, distracted parent

**Role in EaseFlow:** library.
**Use when:** products with multi-step setup, long forms, or tasks that take more than a few minutes.

## Who they are
Sam is a capable professional with two young kids. They start things in 5-minute gaps and get interrupted constantly.

## Tech context
- Devices: switches between phone and laptop.
- Comfort: high, but attention is fragmented.

## What they want
"Let me stop and pick up exactly where I was."

## How they behave
- Leaves mid-flow without warning. Comes back hours or days later, sometimes on another device.
- Forgets what they'd done and what was left.
- Gets irritated when forced to start over.

## Gives up when
- Progress is lost after closing a tab or a session timeout.
- On return, there's nothing to say where they left off.
- A long form has no saving.

## Patience budget
Can leave at **any step** and resume in **one click**, with progress intact, on any device.

## Watch for
- [ ] Is progress saved automatically at every step?
- [ ] Does the product show "continue where you left off" on return?
- [ ] Is there a visible progress indicator ("Step 2 of 4")?
- [ ] Do sessions survive a device switch?

## Pass condition
Reaches the core win across at least two interrupted sessions without redoing anything.

## Voice
> "I was halfway through setting this up yesterday. Now it's showing me the welcome screen again?"
