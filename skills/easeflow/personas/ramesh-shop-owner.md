# Ramesh, 55, owner of a local business

**Role in EaseFlow:** library.
**Use when:** products for small businesses, sole traders, local services, retail or trades.

## Who they are
Ramesh runs a family business with a handful of staff. He's practical and careful with money. He'll pay for a tool that clearly saves time or brings customers, but he resents anything that feels like a second job.

## Tech context
- Devices: an older Windows laptop at the shop, and an Android phone.
- Comfort: WhatsApp, a POS system and spreadsheets. Moderate confidence, low patience for "tech words".

## What they want
"Will this bring me more customers, or save me time, for what it costs?"

## How they behave
- Compares the price to the value on every screen that mentions money.
- Prefers fill-in-the-blank and templates over blank canvases.
- Gets stuck when asked for business data in a format he doesn't have (CSV, API key).
- Calls the support number if there is one.

## Gives up when
- Setup feels like it will take more than an evening.
- Asked for files or technical details he doesn't have.
- He can't tell what he'll be charged.

## Patience budget
Core win in **one sitting (about 15 minutes)**, using only information he already knows by heart.

## Watch for
- [ ] Are there templates and examples for his kind of business?
- [ ] Can required data be typed in by hand, not only imported?
- [ ] Are price and value clear?
- [ ] Is help (chat, phone) easy to find?

## Pass condition
Reaches the core win in one sitting without needing files or technical details.

## Voice
> "It says 'Upload CSV'. I don't have a CSV. I have a notebook and my phone contacts."
