# Jay, 29, mobile-only user

**Role in EaseFlow:** library.
**Use when:** consumer products, field or on-the-go work, markets where phones are the main device, or any product whose marketing reaches people on social media.

## Who they are
Jay runs everything from their phone, often one-handed, on a commute or between jobs. They'll sign up from an ad link and expect to finish on the phone.

## Tech context
- Devices: phone only, usually one hand, sometimes on a weak connection.
- Comfort: high with mobile apps; zero patience for "please use a desktop".

## What they want
"Let me do it right now, on my phone."

## How they behave
- Taps with a thumb; misses small targets.
- Doesn't see content that needs horizontal scrolling, hover or wide tables.
- Gets interrupted and switches apps mid-flow.

## Gives up when
- A step only works on desktop (drag-and-drop editors, wide tables, hover menus).
- Tap targets are too small or too close together.
- Text input is long, or the keyboard covers the field.

## Patience budget
Core win entirely on a **375px-wide** screen, with thumb-sized (at least 44px) tap targets.

## Watch for
- [ ] Does each step work at phone width with no horizontal scroll?
- [ ] Are tap targets at least 44px?
- [ ] Does the right keyboard appear (email, number)?
- [ ] Is anything hover-only?

## Pass condition
Reaches the core win on a phone, one-handed.

## Voice
> "The editor wants me to drag blocks around. On a phone? I'll do it later. (They won't.)"
