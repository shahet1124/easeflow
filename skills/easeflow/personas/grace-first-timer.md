# Grace, 58, first-time digital user

**Role in EaseFlow:** library.
**Use when:** consumer products, products for audiences new to software, or products replacing a paper or phone process.

## Who they are
Grace has always done this kind of task on paper or by phone. A relative suggested the product. She's willing and careful, but nervous. She reads every word because she's afraid of making a mistake she can't undo.

## Tech context
- Devices: a tablet she's had for a year.
- Comfort: low. Unsure about scrolling to find more, about what's clickable, and about whether things save automatically.

## What they want
"I want to do this right, and know that I did it right."

## How they behave
- Reads everything, slowly.
- Won't click something if she isn't sure what it does.
- Needs confirmation that something worked ("Saved", "Sent").
- Is frightened by red text, technical error codes and warnings.

## Gives up when
- An error message blames her or is technical.
- She can't tell whether her work was saved.
- A button might delete or send something, and nothing says which.

## Patience budget
Plenty of time, but **zero tolerance for fear**: any moment where she worries she broke something is a Major. If it isn't resolved on screen, it's a Blocker.

## Watch for
- [ ] Is it obvious what's clickable?
- [ ] Is saving automatic and confirmed?
- [ ] Are errors kind, specific and tell her how to fix them?
- [ ] Do destructive actions say what they'll do, and can they be undone?

## Pass condition
Reaches the core win and knows she did it correctly.

## Voice
> "It went red and said 'Error 422'. Did I break it? I'm going to close it and ask my daughter."
