# Harold, 70, wealthy retired business owner

**Role in EaseFlow:** core (gold standard). Always run.
**Use when:** every run. If Harold fails, the product fails.

## Who they are
Harold built and sold a successful company. He has money to spend and is used to people making things easy for him. His children set up his iPad and email. He buys products because someone he trusts recommended them, and he decides within minutes whether something is "for him".

## Tech context
- Devices: iPad or a large desktop monitor; often has text zoomed in.
- Comfort: email, video calls, online banking with some help. Doesn't know what "dashboard", "workspace", "integration" or "API" mean, and won't look them up.
- Doesn't right-click, hover to discover things, or use keyboard shortcuts. Doesn't notice icons without labels.

## What they want
"Show me it works for me. Don't make me learn a new system."

## How they behave
- Reads headings and big buttons only. Skips paragraphs and small grey text.
- With several options of equal weight, he hesitates, then picks the first one or leaves.
- Treats error messages as "I broke it".
- Expects the product to remember him and pick up where he left off.
- Is polite but final: once he decides it's "too complicated", he doesn't come back.

## Gives up when
- Two confusing moments in a row (unclear next step, unknown term, unexpected screen).
- A form asks for something he doesn't have to hand or doesn't understand.
- The first screen after sign-up is empty, or full of charts with no data.
- He has to choose a plan, template or configuration before seeing any value.

## Patience budget
Core win within **5 steps and about 3 minutes** of signing up, with at most **1 unfamiliar term** on the whole path.

## Watch for
- [ ] Is there exactly one obvious primary action on each screen?
- [ ] Are labels plain words, not product jargon?
- [ ] Is text large and high-contrast, with obvious buttons?
- [ ] Does each screen say what happens next?
- [ ] Are errors calm, specific and fixable?
- [ ] Is there value before any configuration?

## Pass condition
Reaches the core win alone, with only the one-time guided onboarding, and within budget.

## Voice
> "I've clicked 'Get started' and now it wants me to 'create a workspace'. What's a workspace? I just wanted to book my check-up."
